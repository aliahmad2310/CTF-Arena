Competition Rules

  • Flag sharing is prohibited. Do not share, publish, or request flags, partial flag values, challenge solutions, or other information that directly reveals how to claim points. Each participant must solve challenges independently, except for collaboration among members of the same team where team play is supported.
  • Attacks and unauthorized activity are prohibited. Do not conduct denial-of-service or distributed denial-of-service (DoS/DDoS) attacks, social engineering, phishing, malware activity, or any other attack against the platform, its infrastructure, participants, or third-party systems. Only interact with targets explicitly provided for a challenge, and do not disrupt services or access data beyond the challenge scope.
  • Using AI is prohibited. Do not use generative AI tools, chatbots, or AI agents to solve challenges, derive or generate flags, or perform competition tasks on your behalf. Participants remain responsible for their actions and must not use AI agents to carry out prohibited attacks or automate unauthorized platform interactions.
