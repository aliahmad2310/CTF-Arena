CYBER JIRGA HACKTOBERFEST 2026 CTF

Competition Rules

All participants must follow these rules throughout the competition. Violations may result in removal from the event and disqualification.

Participant Conduct

  1. Flag sharing is prohibited

    Do not share, publish, request, or receive flags, partial flag values, or challenge solutions that reveal how to claim points. Participants must solve challenges themselves. Collaboration is permitted only between members of the same team when team play is supported.

  2. Attacks and unauthorized activity are strictly prohibited

    Do not conduct denial-of-service or distributed denial-of-service (DoS/DDoS) attacks, social engineering, phishing, malware activity, or any other attack against the platform, its infrastructure, participants, or third-party systems. Only interact with targets explicitly provided for a challenge. Do not disrupt services, access data outside the challenge scope, or attempt to access accounts or systems without authorization.

  3. Using AI is prohibited

    Do not use generative AI tools, chatbots, or AI agents to solve challenges, derive or generate flags, or perform competition tasks on your behalf.

    Participants remain responsible for their actions and must not use AI agents to carry out prohibited attacks or automate unauthorized platform interactions.

By participating in Cyber Jirga Hacktoberfest 2026 CTF, you agree to follow these rules and respect the competition scope.
